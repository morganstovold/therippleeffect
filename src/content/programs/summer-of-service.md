This summer, kindness becomes an adventure. Summer of Service is a community-wide challenge that encourages kids and teens to complete acts of service, learning, movement, and community connection throughout the summer.

Every completed activity creates a positive ripple in our community, and every completed packet earns entries into prize drawings. The more you complete, the more chances you have to win. Whether you complete a few activities or fill your entire packet, every ripple matters.

Support local, use our parks, and visit our libraries, police stations, and fire departments. Get out, get involved, and be the ripple in Solano County!
