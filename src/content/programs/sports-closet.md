The Ripple Sports Closet was James Baldwin's idea. He noticed that many families have gently used sports equipment sitting in garages while other children miss out on opportunities because of the cost of gear. Today the closet is run by local youth, for youth, through The Ripple Effect of Life.

The program collects new and gently used sports equipment and redistributes it to local children and families who need it most. Items are given out year-round, so every child has the chance to participate in sports, build confidence, stay active, and feel included, regardless of financial barriers.

By keeping usable equipment out of landfills and putting it back into the hands of young athletes, the Ripple Sports Closet creates a cycle of sustainability, accessibility, and community impact.
